Sinigang na Baboy

Description

Sinigang na Baboy is a traditional Filipino sour soup made with pork, vegetables, and tamarind. It has a refreshing sour broth and is usually served hot with steamed rice. It is a favorite comfort food in many Filipino homes.

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram pork belly or pork ribs, cut into pieces
  • 1 packet sinigang mix (about 40 grams), or to taste
  • 8 cups water
  • 1 medium onion, quartered
  • 2 medium tomatoes, quartered
  • 1 piece radish, sliced
  • 1 bunch kangkong (water spinach), washed
  • 8 pieces okra
  • 1 piece eggplant, sliced
  • 2 pieces green finger chilies
  • Fish sauce, to taste

Cooking Instructions

  1. Place the pork and water in a large pot.
  2. Bring to a boil and remove any foam that appears on the surface.
  3. Add the onion and tomatoes.
  4. Lower the heat and simmer for about 45–60 minutes, or until the pork is tender.
  5. Add the radish and eggplant. Cook for about 5 minutes.
  6. Add the okra and green chilies. Cook for another 3–5 minutes.
  7. Add the sinigang mix and stir until dissolved.
  8. Season with fish sauce according to your taste.
  9. Add the kangkong and cook for about 1–2 minutes until wilted.
  10. Turn off the heat and serve hot with steamed rice.

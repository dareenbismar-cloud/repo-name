Featured Filipino Recipes

Explore these delicious Filipino dishes that you can prepare and enjoy with your family.

1. Chicken Adobo

Chicken Adobo is one of the most famous Filipino dishes. It is made with chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaves, and black pepper. The chicken is simmered until tender and flavorful.

View Chicken Adobo Recipe

2. Sinigang

Sinigang is a traditional Filipino sour soup known for its refreshing and delicious taste. It is commonly prepared with pork, vegetables, and tamarind broth.

View Sinigang Recipe

3. Pancit Canton

Pancit Canton is a popular Filipino noodle dish cooked with vegetables, meat, shrimp, and savory seasonings. It is often served during celebrations and family gatherings.

Pancit Canton

Description

Pancit Canton is a popular Filipino noodle dish made with wheat noodles, vegetables, meat, and savory seasonings. It is commonly prepared for birthdays, celebrations, and family gatherings. It is delicious and best served warm.

Ingredients

  • 250 grams pancit canton noodles
  • 200 grams chicken breast, sliced into small pieces
  • 100 grams shrimp, peeled and cleaned (optional)
  • 1 small carrot, sliced into strips
  • 1 cup cabbage, chopped
  • 1 small onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1/2 cup green beans, sliced
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
  • 2 cups chicken broth or water
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • Calamansi for serving (optional)

Cooking Instructions

  1. Heat cooking oil in a large pan or wok over medium heat.
  2. Add the garlic and onion. Cook until fragrant.
  3. Add the sliced chicken and cook until no longer pink and fully cooked.
  4. Add the shrimp, if using, and cook until pink and opaque.
  5. Add the carrots and green beans. Stir-fry for about 2 minutes.
  6. Pour in the chicken broth, soy sauce, and oyster sauce.
  7. Bring the mixture to a boil.
  8. Add the pancit canton noodles and gently mix until they absorb most of the liquid.
  9. Add the cabbage and continue cooking until the noodles are tender and the vegetables are cooked.
  10. Season with salt and black pepper to taste.
  11. Transfer to a serving plate and serve warm with calamansi, if desired.

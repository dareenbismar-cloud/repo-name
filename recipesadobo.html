Chicken Adobo

Description

Chicken Adobo is a popular Filipino dish made with chicken, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, and spices. The chicken is simmered until tender, allowing it to absorb the delicious salty, sour, and savory flavors of the sauce. It is commonly served with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram chicken, cut into serving pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 cup water
  • 6 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 pieces bay leaves
  • 1 teaspoon whole black peppercorns
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional)
  • Steamed rice for serving

Cooking Instructions

  1. Combine the chicken, soy sauce, and crushed garlic in a bowl. Marinate for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a pot over medium heat.
  3. Remove the chicken from the marinade and lightly brown each piece in the pot.
  4. Pour in the marinade, vinegar, and water.
  5. Add the bay leaves and black peppercorns.
  6. Bring the mixture to a boil. Do not stir for the first few minutes after adding the vinegar.
  7. Lower the heat, cover the pot, and simmer for 30–40 minutes or until the chicken is fully cooked and tender.
  8. Add sugar if desired, then adjust the seasoning to taste.
  9. Serve hot with steamed rice.
